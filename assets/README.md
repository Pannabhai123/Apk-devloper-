# Original prototype assets

- `logo.svg` is an original vector shield mark made for this prototype and used in the lobby/project icon.
- Island buildings, terrain patches, cover, aircraft and parachutes, player/bot figures, weapon silhouettes, training targets and Gloo Walls are generated from simple primitive meshes by this repository's scripts.
- Short shot, reload, hit, headshot, pickup, wall, warning and victory effects are synthesized in `scripts/core/audio_manager.gd`; no downloaded sound files or online service are required at runtime.

These are deliberately low-detail prototype assets. They are not copies of proprietary maps, characters, weapon skins, logos or sounds. Godot itself is an external MIT-licensed engine and is not included in this repository.
