# Milestone status and remaining work

This is an interactive Godot prototype, **not a complete production-scale battle royale**. Original content is made procedurally from simple primitives; character models are palette variants, not eight fully modeled, individually animated people. Exposed lobby and HUD actions are wired, but features that do not exist are not presented as finished.

| Milestone | Status | Evidence / remaining work |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 — engine, Android support, movement, camera, test build | **Partial** | Godot project, landscape export preset, touch and desktop movement/camera exist. Headless simulations run. Android build and phone touch validation remain blocked by missing SDK/JDK/templates and hardware. |
| 2 — shooting, AI, pickup, reload | **Prototype implemented** | Twelve firearm configurations, head/body physics rays, armor/health, bots, pickup, reload, hit feedback and tests. Production-quality models, animations, audio and collision tuning remain. |
| 3 — map, loot, zone, victory, deployment | **Partial** | Approximately 320 × 320 m test island, ten small named landmarks, bounded shrinking zone and last-survivor rules. Battle has a fixed aircraft path, timed bot departures, steerable parachutes, automatic drop and physical landing; practice and training remain ground starts. This is not the requested full 1.5 km island or a polished deployment sequence. |
| 4 — Gloo Wall, weapons, healing, minimap | **Prototype implemented** | Confirmable solid cover, healing, 12 firearms, three functional optics, minimap and cover-aware frag grenades. Ground-level Gloo Walls dynamically change the bot navigation grid. No smoke/flash, limb hitboxes or broader attachment system yet. |
| 5 — characters, settings, audio, saves | **Partial** | Eight palette-based operators with distinct timed/cooldown abilities, a draggable HUD editor with presets, functional settings, synthesized effects and local saves. ## Explicit omissions and limitations

- **Content:** no full 1.5 km island, ten production-quality locations, water/bridges, high-fidelity tactical models, skeletal animations, licensed music/voice, weapon skins or unlockable outfits. The ten landmarks are small primitive compounds, not finished points of interest. Most structures are open-front shells; only the watchpost has a usable rooftop ramp. Battle's aircraft, canopy, path and landing are simplified and not yet visually or device-tested.
- **Combat and equipment:** twelve firearms in six roles, one frag type and three optics are implemented. There is no smoke/flash grenade, melee, crossbow, helmets, prone, limb hitboxes, bullet ballistics or bullet damage falloff. Grenades use radial falloff and cover checks. Optics affect FOV/spread and draw basic reticles, but there are no dedicated sight/scope models or full scope views.
- **Characters:** abilities have real effects and cooldowns, but operators share primitive geometry and simple procedural motion. No unique skeletal rigs, outfit unlocks or professionally balanced abilities exist.
- **AI:** easy/normal/hard/expert alter reaction time and spread. Bots use sight, approximate bounded gunshot hearing, brief last-seen memory, a shared 128 × 128 ground grid, local collision probes, loot, healing, grenade/cover use and ability decisions. Ground-level Gloo Wall occupancy is added and removed dynamically; rooftop cover does not incorrectly block the floor below. Recon temporarily senses nearby enemies through cover; firing still requires sight. There is no navigation mesh, multi-level path planning, sophisticated cover planning, distant simulation LOD or fully tactical state machine. Bots can still get stuck on complex geometry. The 49-bot setting is a functional upper limit, not a validated mobile target.
- **Controls/settings:** individual touch positions, default/compact presets, mirroring, size and opacity are implemented. Gyroscope, granular audio buses, language selection, motion blur, anti-aliasing controls and the complete sensitivity/scope matrix are absent. Simulated multi-touch and widescreen coordinate checks do not replace physical-device testing.
- **Modes/progression:** matches are not guaranteed to last 8–15 minutes; the safe zone and bot density may finish them sooner. Practice reuses the same map. Training has unlimited reserve ammo and four targets, not the requested full practice suite. Missions are one-time local milestones, not a daily rotation. Cosmetics and characters are all available from the start.
- **Quality assurance:** no visual/device playtest, Android lifecycle verification, sustained FPS/battery/memory measurements, production release signing, install or launch evidence exists. Any Actions debug APK must not be mistaken for a production release. See [TEST_REPORT.md](TEST_REPORT.md).

## Recommended next steps

1. Continue map and content production incrementally: larger navigable terrain, detailed locations, more real interiors/elevation, authored assets, and testable equipment rather than decorative menu items.
2. Improve multi-level bot navigation, tactics and perception; add broader seeded bot-vs-bot, long-match and balance tests.
3. Set up the documented Android toolchain when ready; build a debug APK, verify signature/manifest, and test full offline matches on representative phones.
4. Fix touch, visual, audio and camera issues found on hardware; profile frame time and memory with 3, 10 and 49 bots before choosing device-specific limits.
5. Add full lifecycle, corrupt-save, device-resolution and accessibility QA before any release; independently review game name/trademark and content rating.
